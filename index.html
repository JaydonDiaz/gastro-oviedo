Featured Procedures

Our most requested procedures

Colonoscopy

A thin, flexible scope lets your doctor examine the lining of the colon to screen for colorectal cancer and investigate symptoms like bleeding, chronic diarrhea, or a change in bowel habits. Most patients are sedated and comfortable throughout.

Call to schedule

Upper Endoscopy (EGD)

Upper endoscopy lets your doctor examine the esophagus, stomach, and duodenum to evaluate reflux, ulcers, swallowing difficulty, and unexplained abdominal pain — usually in well under an hour, start to finish.

For Patients

Planning your first visit?

Here's what to know before your appointment or procedure.

Procedures

Most of our procedures are performed at the University Surgery Center in Winter Park, FL — just 11 minutes from our office. If you're having a procedure there, please complete their Pre-Assessment Form ahead of your visit.

Insurance

We accept Medicare and most private insurance plans. We're unable to accept Medicaid at this time. Call our office and we'll gladly confirm your coverage before your visit.

What to Bring

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any relevant records from a referring physician to your first appointment.
